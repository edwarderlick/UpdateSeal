## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conviction, authoritative visual voice for decentralized governance and autonomous software verification. Borrowing structural cues from physical manifestos, European editorial broadsheets, and technical registry prints, the system rejects the soft, blurred tropes of conventional Web3 and SaaS interfaces in favor of razor-sharp geometric tension, graphic immediacy, and uncompromising cryptographic utility.

### Brand Personality & Voice
- **Rigorous & Immutable:** Unforgiving structural borders and stark mathematical layouts communicate total auditability and zero tolerance for failure.
- **Graphic & Architectural:** Asymmetrical grid lockups, stepped jagged sectional masks, and staggered modular blocks signal engineered precision rather than decorative styling.
- **Editorial Directness:** Massive condensed headlines paired with compact monospace metadata labels emulate industrial printing and legal gazettes.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Stark Geometric Brutalism with Editorial Refinement:** Absolute 0px border radii, hairline dividers, stepped silhouettes, and sharp rectangular containers.
- **Color-Coded Semantic Accents:** Vivid functional cards (pastel yellow, muted rose, electric cyan) punctuate deep field tones to denote multi-party governance, permission thresholds, and cryptographic state transitions.
- **Annotation & Stamp Metaphor:** Tilted chip labels, monospace index counters (`[01]`, `[02]`), and micro-stickers applied across headlines echo physical validation stamps and approval seals.

## Colors

The palette balances broad tactile warmth with high-visibility warning tones and deep archival green surfaces. It relies on decisive color blocking rather than gradients or soft drop-shadows.

### Color Archetypes
- **Base Canvas (`#F5F2EB`):** A warm, tactile ecru/cream foundational surface that evokes raw broadsheet paper and architectural boards.
- **Primary Sovereign (`#102A1E`):** An intense, near-black forest green used for heavy structural containers, cryptographic proof modules, footer foundations, and key data panels.
- **Alert / Action Accent (`#F25C19`):** A radiant international orange reserved for critical calls to action, pending consensus states, interactive highlights, and urgent system banners.
- **Registry Categorical Accents:**
  - **Audit Yellow (`#F6E97E`):** Used for threshold summaries, security status stickers, and active card panels.
  - **Consensus Coral/Pink (`#FFAAA6`):** Dedicated to quorum approvals, autonomous agent signature states, and protocol notices.
  - **Telemetry Cyan (`#79E2F2`):** Deployed for smart contract hashes, on-chain execution states, and data telemetry.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for high-contrast data matrices, clear metrics cards, and inner text fields embedded inside dark or tinted blocks.
- **Ink Black (`#0E1512`):** Deep carbon typography tone ensuring crisp readability against ecru, white, and pastel card surfaces.

### Application Rules
- Never blend or gradient these colors; apply them strictly as hard-edged solids.
- Backgrounds transition abruptly using hard horizontal division lines or stepped geometric cutouts.
- When placing text on dark forest green (`#102A1E`) or alert orange (`#F25C19`), type must render in crisp pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or pale ecru (`#F5F2EB`).

## Typography

Typography delivers brutalist, editorial authority through stark contrast between structural display type, neutral technical body copy, and mechanical monospace indexing labels.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Behavior
- **Headlines (`Syne`):** Heavyweight, condensed, muscular display headers. Render titles tight with negative letter-spacing and compact leading. Headline blocks frequently integrate inline sticker tags tilted slightly to offset the severe rectilinear grid.
- **Body & Continuous Reading (`Chivo`):** A sharp, modern grotesque with distinct technical proportions. Retains legibility at small sizes across dark and light surfaces without compromising the clinical tone.
- **Labels, Telemetry & Code (`Space Mono`):** Monospaced type handles index numbers (`[01]`, `04/12`), protocol parameters, cryptographic hashes, and micro-category tags. Always rendered with positive letter-spacing and uppercase styling.

### Editorial Annotation Stickers
Inline headline accents employ `label-sm` or `label-md` enclosed in solid rectangular badge pills rotated between `-2.5deg` and `+3deg`. These emulate rapid physical review stamps ("Release Verified", "Multi-Sig OK", "Agent 0x4F").

## Layout & Spacing

The structural framework is built on a tight 12-column architectural grid with structural border lines running edge-to-edge. Spacing is rhythmic, rigid, and dense, eliminating ambiguous floating gaps.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** A wide 12-column layout defined by `1.5rem` gutters and minimum `2.5rem` outer canvas margins. Multi-column cards span distinct asymmetric units (e.g., 5-col split alongside 7-col stepped imagery).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Collapses complex asymmetric splits into equal pairs or 4+4 distributions, maintaining a `1rem` gutter.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Converts stepped layouts into stacked vertical blocks with `1.25rem` outer gutters. Elements stretch to full width, retaining hard rectangular boundaries.

### Stepped and Asymmetric Geometry
- Large background panels employ hard geometric notches and diagonal steps (e.g., polygon clip paths or staggered rectangular blocks) to transition between the ecru base and dark forest or orange panels.
- Content groupings and metric callouts sit within segmented grid cells enclosed by `1px` solid borders, functioning as a continuous unified data ledger.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids ambient Gaussian blurs, floating shadows, and neumorphic extrusions. Depth is produced purely through surface layering, structural line boundaries, and stark color blocking.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor:** `#F5F2EB` (Ecru/Cream canvas) forms the standard broadsheet plane.
- **Architectural Slabs:** `#102A1E` (Dark Forest) and `#F25C19` (Vibrant Orange) sit directly on the ground plane as flat structural zones, demarcating operational modules and registry entries.
- **Floating Accent Tiles:** Cards finished in `#F6E97E`, `#FFAAA6`, and `#79E2F2` sit directly on high-contrast backgrounds without shadow offsets.

### Structural Borders
- All element separation relies on crisp `1px` or `2px` solid ink outlines (`#0E1512` on light planes, `#FFFFFF` or `#234937` on dark planes).
- Visual hierarchy is established by altering border weight, container background color, and font scale rather than z-index elevation.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary is uncompromisingly rectilinear. Every component, button, badge, input, card, and image frame uses a `0px` border radius (`roundedness: 0`).

### Shape Philosophy
- **Zero Radius:** No rounded corners, soft pills, or squishy buttons. Every edge terminates at an exact 90-degree right angle.
- **Stepped Edges:** Complex hero containers, badges, and image frames occasionally incorporate stepped corner cutouts (staircase stepped pixels) to visually reinforce digital assembly and on-chain immutability.
- **Square Aspect Dividers:** Visual bullet points and section indicators use pure filled squares (`6px × 6px` or `8px × 8px`) rather than standard circles.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Controls
- **Primary CTA:** Compact rectangular block with a solid `#FFFFFF` or `#102A1E` background, framed by sharp 90-degree corners. Typographic styling uses `label-md` or `headline-sm` in all-caps or title case, followed by a small directional arrow (`→` or `↗`). Internal padding: `space-sm` vertically by `space-md` horizontally.
- **Inverted Action Button:** Solid `#F25C19` orange button with white typography and a sharp dark trailing arrow, used for irreversible on-chain execution (e.g., "Seal Release →").
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px` solid `#0E1512` or `#FFFFFF` border, high-contrast hover state that fills the background with the opposing tone.

### Metric & Status Cards
- **Editorial Pastel Accent Cards:** Rectangular containers with solid fills of Pale Yellow (`#F6E97E`), Soft Pink (`#FFAAA6`), or Electric Cyan (`#79E2F2`). Each card features:
  - Top-left index stamp (e.g., `[01]` or icon).
  - Bold condensed title in `Syne` (`headline-md`).
  - Monospace status parameter or short summary in `body-sm`.
  - Zero radius, crisp `1px` dark perimeter border.
- **Registry Stat Blocks:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) or dark forest (`#102A1E`) square grid tiles presenting oversized metric figures (e.g., `99.98%`, `500+`) paired with small all-caps monospace labels beneath.

### Rotated Annotation Stickers & Chips
- Miniature badges placed at angles (`-2deg` to `+3deg`) overlapping headlines, card headers, or image borders.
- Background fills strictly utilize accent tones: `#F25C19` (Orange), `#F6E97E` (Yellow), or `#FFAAA6` (Pink).
- Text rendered in `label-sm` Space Mono, ink-black or pure white, uppercase with zero radius.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Flat rectangular field with an ecru or white background, enclosed by a `1px` or `2px` solid `#0E1512` border.
- Placeholder text in subdued dark green or ink black (`body-md`), with a tight attached rectangular submit button nestled flush within the same boundary.

### Interactive Lists & Row Selectors
- Border-bottom rows spanning the full column width.
- Hover states transition the row background instantly to `#102A1E` or `#F25C19`, inverting text color to white with an animated corner arrow (`↗`) shifting `4px` diagonally.
